Understanding GBV

Gender-Based Violence (GBV) is a serious issue affecting many individuals in Kenya. It includes physical, emotional, and psychological abuse.

Causes of GBV

  • Patriarchal societal norms
  • Poverty and economic dependence
  • Lack of strict legal enforcement

How to Combat GBV

  1. Education and awareness
  2. Stronger legal frameworks
  3. Support for survivors
